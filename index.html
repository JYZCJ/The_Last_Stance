RULES

HOW THE DUEL WORKS

01 ESSENTIALS

  • 2 players enter the duel.
  • The battle takes place on a 5x5 board.
  • Each player starts with 8 HP.
  • If both fighters reach 0 HP at the same time, the match ends in a draw.

05 THE POISON ZONE

  • Starting in Round 3, the outer ring becomes poisonous.
  • Only the inner 3x3 area remains safe.
  • Poison is checked at the end of each round.
  • Rounds 3–4 deal 1 poison damage.
  • Rounds 5–6 deal 2 poison damage.
  • Round 7 onward deals 3 poison damage.

02 ROUND STRUCTURE

  • Draw 3 attack cards.
  • Openly take movement cards for the round.
  • Secretly choose 3 actions.
  • Place all 3 actions face down.
  • Reveal both turns at the same time.
  • Resolve Action 1, then Action 2, then Action 3.

06 CARDS AND ACTIONS

  • Each player uses a 14-card attack deck.
  • The deck contains Slash, Heavy Strike, Lunge, Sweep, Riposte, Push, and Block.
  • At the end of the round, you may keep 1 unused attack card for the next round.
  • Each fighter also keeps 3 permanent Stay cards that are never discarded.

03 COMMITMENT AND REVEAL

  • Both players lock in three actions before the round begins.
  • Actions are revealed simultaneously.
  • Every exchange is resolved at the same time.
  • Both players can strike, miss, or suffer damage in the same moment.

07 CORE PRINCIPLES

  • If both attacks are valid in the same action, both players take damage.
  • A fighter may move into an attack path and still be hit during that same action.
  • Positioning, timing, and commitment matter just as much as raw damage.

04 MOVEMENT AND CONFLICT

  • Each player has a public pool of 5 movement cards.
  • Your opponent knows how many movement cards you took, but not when you will use them.
  • If both fighters try to enter the same space, both remain where they are.
  • If both fighters try to swap spaces, both also stay in place.
